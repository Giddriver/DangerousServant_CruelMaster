# Dangerous Servant / Cruel Master

A turn-based strategy game. Open `index.html` in any browser. No install or build.

You hold an Illithid in a cell beneath your garret and use it to dominate people across six institutions of Caldreth. Each thrall occupies your mind strength. The Illithid pushes every night and sometimes surges.

The Illithid grows stronger over time, and faster on any night you do not work it, so waiting loses. The Magister's Inquisitor searches the city for your thralls. Reach 100 Subversion with the Collegium's Provost held, then confront the High Magister at the Spire before the Illithid outgrows your mind.
